ACME STORE — CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
============================================

RETURNS & REFUNDS
-----------------
Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unused and in original packaging. To start a return, email support@acmestore.com with your order number.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. The amount will be credited to your original payment method.

Q: Can I return a sale item?
A: Sale items marked as FINAL SALE cannot be returned. All other sale items follow the standard 30-day return policy.

Q: What if my item arrived damaged?
A: If your item arrived damaged, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will send a replacement at no cost to you.

SHIPPING
--------
Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to over 50 countries. International shipping typically takes 7-14 business days. Customs fees are the responsibility of the customer.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking email. You can also log into your account and view "Order History" to see live tracking.

Q: Is free shipping available?
A: Yes! Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping within the US.

ACCOUNT & ORDERS
----------------
Q: How do I cancel my order?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placing them. Go to My Account → Orders → Cancel Order. After 1 hour, the order is sent to our warehouse and cannot be cancelled.

Q: I forgot my password, how do I reset it?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email. You will receive a reset link within 5 minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
A: Address changes can be made within 1 hour of ordering by contacting support@acmestore.com. After that, the order cannot be modified.

Q: How do I apply a discount code?
A: Enter your discount code in the "Promo Code" field at checkout before completing payment. Only one code can be used per order.

PRODUCTS
--------
Q: Are your products under warranty?
A: All products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty against defects. This does not cover accidental damage or misuse.

Q: How do I know if an item is in stock?
A: If the "Add to Cart" button is visible, the item is in stock. Items that show "Notify Me" are currently out of stock.

Q: Do you restock sold-out items?
A: Most items are restocked within 2-4 weeks. Click "Notify Me" on the product page to receive an email when it's back in stock.

PAYMENTS
--------
Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay.

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and never store your full card details. All payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor.

Q: Why was my payment declined?
A: Payments may be declined due to incorrect card details, insufficient funds, or your bank flagging the transaction. Try again or contact your bank. If the issue persists, email support@acmestore.com.
