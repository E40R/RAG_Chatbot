FABRIQ HRMS — HELP DOCUMENTATION
================================
A guide to using the fabrIQ HRMS: roles, modules, and how to do the common
things you will need day to day. Throughout this guide "you" means the person
logged in, and "your subordinates" means the people who report to you.


ROLES & ACCESS (Role Based Access Control)
------------------------------------------
HRMS has Role Based Access Control which gives different views to different
roles/templates. Each template has different capabilities. (Role here doesn't
mean your company assigned role, but HRMS specific roles like manager that
grant approvals such as reimbursements requested by your subordinates.) These
are configurable by System Admin and HR.

Templates:
- Employees (default): self service capabilities like apply leave, profile edit.
- Manager: sub-organisation view, direct reporters' approvals, and other
  capabilities.
- Leadership: special configuration permissions, entire organisation wide access.
- Team Lead: views and actions scoped to the team.
- HR: configuration settings, policy updates, documents request and view.

Across every module (from Employees onwards) you will see the same structure:
- My Space: actions and views specific to you, which don't involve your
  subordinates' actions.
- Subordinates: reviewing or approving your subordinates' requests (for example
  approving their leave, correcting their attendance). Visible to Manager,
  Team Lead, and above.
- Configure: module specific settings, accessible by Leadership, HR, and
  System Admin.


LIST OF MODULES

1) Login

5) Leaves & OD

1) LOGIN
--------
You can log in with your email id and password, or quickly log in by selecting
"Sign in with Microsoft".

If you forgot your password:
- Type your email.
- Click "Forgot password".
- You will then get a mail to your registered email with further instructions.


2) HOME MODULE
--------------
Home is the first thing you would see after you log in. You can select your
location and mark your attendance for the day quickly. Marking attendance
records one half of the day; the other half needs an approved half-day leave,
otherwise it is counted as absent.

There are two parts to Home: Actions and Widgets.
- Actions: These are the actions predicted to be most visited across the other
  modules, placed directly on Home so you can quickly do that action (apply
  leave, etc.) without going to the other module. The current timestamp and
  location will be logged.
- Widgets: These primarily show live information and HRMS tasks, with some
  actions that can be performed in a few clicks right from the Home screen. You
  can add or remove widgets by selecting the "Edit home screen" option.


3) EMPLOYEES MODULE
-------------------
My Space:
- Employee Directory: Access emails and positions of all employees in fabrIQ,
  filter by department and location. You can email them directly and also view
  them in the org chart.
- Org Chart: The organisation chart gives a bird's-eye view of fabrIQ.

Configure (Leadership, HR, System Admin):
- Add Employee: Employees can be added by filling in the mandatory full name,
  email, and phone number. You can select a template and reporting manager and
  other optional fields, or leave them for the employee to add later. Employees
  can edit their details in My Profile.
- Org Builder: The organisation structure can be organised here. Remove a
  relation by clicking the line connecting two people, or add a connection by
  clicking a node and dragging to the reporter's node. Employees can be quickly
  added by clicking the plus icon. Changes are saved on clicking Save. Click
  "Auto arrange" to tidy the org chart.
- Activation Request: System Admin and other templates have the option to
  disable an employee, which is reversible. The employee gets a notification and
  won't be able to access their HRMS. They can request activation, which will be
  shown here in Activation Requests.


4) ATTENDANCE MODULE
--------------------
You can mark your attendance on the Home screen, which will be reflected here.
You can correct your attendance, in case you forgot to mark it, within 2 days.
The smallest unit is a half day. You can select an optional holiday in either
the Attendance module or the Holiday section — both make the same change. Click
a date marked present or half day to see hours spent and other details.

Colour key:
- Green: present
- Red: absent (this will be converted to LOP unless addressed)
- Blue: holiday
- Golden star: floating / optional holiday

Regularise: review days marked absent and either:
- Request for leave.
- Request for correction (if it's older than 2 days, your manager's approval is
  needed; it auto-approves after 7 days).
If left unattended, the day will be considered LOP (Loss Of Pay) after 35 days
from that day.

Subordinates:
- Attendance Board: review your subordinates' attendance, and mark and view it
  in a calendar view.
- Correction Request: review requests made by your subordinates and either
  approve or reject with a reason that will be shown to them.

Configure: Visible to HR and System Admin, to edit holidays, set weekdays, etc.


5) LEAVES & OD MODULE
---------------------
How to apply for leave:
You can apply for a full or half day leave in two ways.

Way 1 — List view (step by step):
1. Go to the Leaves & OD module.
2. Select the start date and the end date.
3. Select the leave type (currently all are casual leaves; once the fabrIQ leave
   policy is finalised, leave types can be configured in Settings).
4. Select the duration (full day or half day) and add an optional reason.
5. Review the Leave Summary — it shows your available leaves and the leaves
   remaining after deducting the ones you just selected, so you can check before
   applying. (This summary is specific to the leave type selected; the Leave
   Report shows the balance and summary for all leave types.)
6. Click Apply.

Way 2 — Calendar view (quick apply):
1. Open the Calendar view.
2. Click the start date and drag to the end date.
3. Select the duration and leave type.
4. Click Apply.

You can also review holidays and plan your leave in the same place without
switching tabs.

My Space:
- Apply On Duty (OD): On Duty needs to be applied when you work at a client site
  or a location other than your designated office. A request is sent to your
  manager and, upon approval, your attendance is corrected.
- Leave Review: review your applied leaves (also possible under "Requests in
  progress" in Apply Leave). Withdraw them before your manager approves, or
  cancel them after approval, or access your leave history. Cancelling an
  approved leave needs your manager's confirmation.

Subordinates:
- Leave Review: approve or reject your subordinates' leaves, with a reason.
- Leave Tracking: review the leave calendar to see who will be on leave on
  certain days. Review individual leaves month by month.


6) REIMBURSEMENTS MODULE
------------------------
Apply for and review reimbursement claims. There are three types of
reimbursements:
- Receipt based: needs a mandatory file upload.
- Without Receipt: no file upload needed.
- Travel based: reimbursement is calculated from the per-km cost set up in
  Settings and the km you enter. Multiple dates can be selected.

Your submissions can be reviewed in the "My Submissions" tab.

Managers can review and approve their subordinates' claims in the "Pending
Review" tab under Subordinates, and see all reimbursement claims made in "All
History".


7) DOCUMENTS MODULE
-------------------
Upload documents, or request documents from a specific employee or from
everyone. Certain pre-defined document types are provided (based on common
references) so requests stay consistent. Documents and requests can be reviewed
in the "My Documents" section.

My Space:
- My Documents: view documents you have uploaded and documents shared with you,
  and see the status of any documents that have been requested from you (pending
  or submitted).
- Upload / Submit: upload a document against a request, or store a personal
  document such as an ID proof or certificate.

Subordinates / HR:
- Request Documents: request one or more document types from a specific
  employee or from everyone at once, so you can collect what is needed in one
  place.
- Review: review submitted documents and mark them as accepted, or ask for a
  re-upload with a reason.


8) SETTINGS MODULE
------------------
Accessible only to HR, System Admin, and Leadership roles. Various
configurations can be done here, such as updating the leave policy, adding
default leaves, setting the per-km cost used for travel reimbursements, and
similar organisation-wide settings.


9) MY PROFILE
-------------
My Profile is your personal record in the HRMS, and it is where the details
other modules rely on come from — so keeping it up to date matters. It is
available to every employee for their own profile.

Here you can:
- View and edit your personal details: full name, contact number, email, and
  other optional fields. Fields left blank when your account was created (for
  example by HR while adding you) can be completed here.
- See your work details: your template/role, reporting manager, department, and
  location, so you know how you sit in the organisation. Role and reporting
  manager are managed by HR / System Admin via the Employees module rather than
  edited directly here.
- Reach the information the other modules use: your attendance, leave balance,
  reimbursements, and documents all tie back to the identity shown on your
  profile.

Keeping your profile accurate means approvals, notifications, and reports reach
the right place, so review it whenever your contact details change.
